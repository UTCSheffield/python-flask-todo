```mermaid
mindmap
  Root["Start Your Web App Design"]

    Explore["Explore Possibilities"]
      Problems["What annoys or frustrates you day-to-day?"]
      Enjoyment["What do you love spending time on? Why?"]
      Imagine["If you could wave a magic wand, what digital tool would you invent?"]
    WhatIf["What If...?"]
      NoLimits["What if there were no limitations—what would you build?"]
      Superpower["What unique thing could your web app do?"]
    Entities["Think About Entities"]
      Things["What are the things (entities) involved?"]
      Relationships["How do these things connect to each other?"]
      Properties["What qualities or info does each thing have?"]
    Data["About Data"]
      Privacy["How can you protect user privacy?"]
      Personal["Is it possible to help people without storing personal info?"]
      Sources["Where does your app's data come from? Who creates or manages it?"]
    Next["What’s the next question you'd ask yourself?"]
```

## Decide on a name & simple design

* **Name** What is it called?
* **Purpose** What is it for? 
	* What problem are you trying to solve?
	* Who needs it and what are there needs?
* **Success Criteria** What does Success look like?
	* How will you know it works?
* **Analysis** 
	* **Entities** What thing will you store info about?
	* **Inputs**, **Outputs**, **Processing** & **Storage**?
